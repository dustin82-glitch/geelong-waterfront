Sail the Geelong Waterfront

Sparkling Corio Bay, a lively promenade and one of Victoria's best sailing destinations — all minutes from the city centre.

Sailing on Corio Bay

Geelong is a sailing city. Sheltered waters, steady breezes and a calendar full of regattas make Corio Bay a favourite for beginners and racers alike.

🏁

Royal Geelong Yacht Club

One of Australia's oldest yacht clubs, running weekly racing and major regattas from Geelong's waterfront.

🎓

Learn to Sail

Local clubs and sailing schools offer taster days and courses for kids and adults.

🚤

Marinas & Moorings

Berths, ramps and boat hire close to the Esplanade make it easy to get out on the water.

🌅

Watch from Shore

Grab a coffee on the promenade and watch spinnakers fill as fleets race across the bay.

Events

Typical highlights on the waterfront calendar (confirm current dates with organisers).

  • JanuaryGeelong Week regatta – Australia's oldest annual regatta on Corio Bay.
  • SummerTwilight racing, outdoor cinema and live music on the foreshore.
  • SummerCadel Evans Great Ocean Road Race finishes in Geelong.
  • SpringMarkets and community festivals along the Esplanade.
  • Year-roundFood trucks, festivals and sailing club open days.

More Great Things to Do

🎠

Carousel

Ride the heritage carousel at Steampacket Gardens.

🗿

Baywalk Bollards

Follow Jan Mitchell's colourful painted bollards along the foreshore.

🏊

Eastern Beach

Swim at the art-deco beach, promenade and sea baths.

🍽️

Dine by the Bay

Waterfront cafés, bars and seafood just off the pier.

🚴

Walk & Cycle

Flat, scenic paths run along the bay foreshore.

🎡

Family Fun

Playgrounds, mini golf and Cunningham Pier lookouts.

Plan Your Visit

Geelong is about an hour from Melbourne by train (V/Line to Geelong Station, a short walk to the waterfront) or by car via the M1. Parking is available along the Esplanade and Eastern Beach Road.
