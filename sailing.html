Ways to get on the water

Whether you want to race, learn or just cruise, there is a way in.

🏁

Club racing

Weekly keelboat and dinghy racing run by local clubs through summer and winter.

Club calendars
🎓

Learn to sail

Clubs and schools run taster days and courses for kids and adults. Ask your nearest club.

🤝

Crew up

Many skippers welcome new crew. Turn up at a club on race day and introduce yourself.

🚤

Trailer sailing

Launch from the ramp and sail with the trailable yacht community.

🌅

Watch from shore

Coffee on the promenade, spinnakers on the bay. Free front row seats.

Season at a glance

  • SummerTwilight and weekend racing

    Long days, sea breezes and the big regattas.

  • AutumnPointscore finals

    Series wrap up with cooler, steadier conditions.

  • WinterWinter series

    Hardy fleets keep racing. Great for experienced crews.

  • SpringPre-season and learn-to-sail

    Boats come out, courses open and new crew join.
